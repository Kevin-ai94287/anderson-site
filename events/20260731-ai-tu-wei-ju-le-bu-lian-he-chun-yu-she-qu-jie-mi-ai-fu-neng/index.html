AI Breakthrough Club and Chunyu Community Unveil the Path to AI-Empowered OPC Implementation

July 2026

AI Breakthrough Club and Chunyu Community co-hosted the 'Yunling Youth Innovation, AI Empowers' open class, exploring how AI can empower the real implementation of OPC. The event was packed with practical insights, helping entrepreneurs master AI tools.

AI Breakthrough Club, together with Chunyu Community, focused on a core proposition during the 'Yunling Youth Innovation, AI Empowers' event: how to empower the real implementation of OPC in the AI era.

Why Discuss OPC Implementation

OPC (Online Personal Entrepreneurship) is a choice many people make today, but few truly succeed. The reason lies not in AI technology itself, but in how to embed AI tools into business processes to improve efficiency at every step. This open class addressed the issue head-on.

Three Actionable Main Lines

Xiao'an Laosi shared three main lines for OPC implementation on site: using AI to gain customer insights, using AI to optimize products, and using AI for batch customer acquisition. Each line came with specific operational steps, not just empty talk about trends. Participants could try them out right after the session.

Interactive Q&A: Real Questions Get Real Answers

During the Q&A session, attendees raised very specific concerns, such as 'How can AI-generated content avoid homogenization?' and 'How can small teams use AI at low cost?' Xiao'an Laosi responded to each, turning general methodologies into directly applicable tips.

The True Value of an Open Class

An open class is not about instilling knowledge but igniting action. The collaboration between AI Breakthrough Club and Chunyu Community aims precisely to help more entrepreneurs find a foothold for AI implementation. If you are also exploring AI-empowered business, start with a real problem and put the methods into practice.
