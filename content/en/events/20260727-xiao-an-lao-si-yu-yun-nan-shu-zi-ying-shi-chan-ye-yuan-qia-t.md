## Xiao An Laosi Discusses AIGC Empowerment Cooperation with Yunnan Digital Film and Television Industrial Park

Recently, Xiao An Laosi visited the Yunnan Digital Film and Television Industrial Park to discuss AIGC training and industry empowerment with park leaders. The two sides engaged in in-depth exchanges on topics such as the application prospects of AI technology in digital film and television, talent cultivation, and technology implementation.

![Article image 1](/media/xiao-an-lao-si-yu-yun-nan-shu-zi-ying-shi-chan-ye-yuan-qia-t/4d25fd1771374fcdaf76a0ffdcfe2524.jpg)

### AIGC: A New Engine for the Film and Television Industry

During the meeting, Xiao An Laosi pointed out that AIGC technology is reshaping the film production process, from pre-production planning and scriptwriting to post-production special effects, editing, and distribution, with AI providing efficient assistance. The park leaders highly agreed and shared the park's layout and needs in digital content production.

### Cooperation Direction: Training and Industry Empowerment

Both sides agreed that the current film and television industry urgently needs composite talents who understand both content and AI. Xiao An Laosi proposed customizing AIGC training courses for the park, covering modules such as tool operation, process optimization, and creative application. Additionally, the two sides explored deeper cooperation possibilities in project incubation and technical consulting.

### Looking Ahead

This discussion laid a solid foundation for future cooperation. Xiao An Laosi will continue to communicate with the Yunnan Digital Film and Television Industrial Park to promote the early implementation of AIGC training and empowerment projects, helping to drive high-quality development of the regional digital film and television industry.
