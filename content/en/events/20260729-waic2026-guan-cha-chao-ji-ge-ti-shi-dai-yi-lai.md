## WAIC2026 Observations: The Era of the Super Individual Has Arrived

Recently, Xiao An Lao Si gave a talk in the Am community sharing observations from the WAIC2026 Artificial Intelligence Conference, with the theme "This is the best era for super individuals." Here are the key points.

![Article image 1](/media/waic2026-guan-cha-chao-ji-ge-ti-shi-dai-yi-lai/904c7617881244a189af2b73c7751956.jpg)
![Article image 3](/media/waic2026-guan-cha-chao-ji-ge-ti-shi-dai-yi-lai/474e606040594c849a6d9eb6d52fd9a8.jpg)

### Large Models Go Mainstream
At this year's WAIC, almost all vendors are lowering the barrier to AI usage. Users no longer need programming knowledge; they can simply use natural language to call upon large models to complete tasks. This means AI is no longer exclusive to a select few.

### Intelligent Agents Become a New Trend
Multiple exhibitors launched intelligent agent platforms capable of autonomously completing multi-step tasks. For example, input "Help me plan a weekend trip," and the AI will automatically check the weather, book hotels, and generate an itinerary. Xiao An Lao Si believes such tools will fundamentally change individual work efficiency.

### Democratization of Creative Tools
AI painting, AI music, and AI video generation tools were a hit on-site. Ordinary users can produce professional-grade work in minutes. Creativity is no longer a talent reserved for a few; it's an ability everyone can achieve with AI.

### Q&A Session
- Q: How should ordinary people get started? A: Choose a specific scenario, such as writing a weekly report or making a poster, and experiment repeatedly with AI tools.
- Q: Will AI replace humans? A: No, but those who use AI will replace those who don't. The key is proactive learning and practice.

Conclusion: Xiao An Lao Si encourages everyone to seize the dividends of this era and start arming themselves with AI today. Share this with more people, and together, let's become super individuals.
