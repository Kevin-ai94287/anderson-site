## AI Office 3.0: Industry Trends from WAIC

Recently, Xiao'an Laosi hosted an AI Office 3.0 open class at the Xishan Zijianshan OPC community, sharing firsthand observations from the WAIC2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference. The event targeted ordinary people interested in AI applications, focusing on entrepreneurial opportunities revealed at the conference.

## Three Major Trends

Xiao'an Laosi visited small startup booths at the conference and summarized three trends:
- **Scenario-based**: AI is no longer generic but deeply integrated into specific office scenarios.
- **Embodied**: More physical robot products with intuitive interaction methods.
- **Application-oriented**: Lowering the barrier to entry so ordinary people can quickly get started.

## Low-Cost Entrepreneurship Path

Xiao'an Laosi broke down how ordinary people can build a one-person company based on their own abilities. Key steps:
1. Identify personal value offerings (e.g., writing, design, consulting).
2. Use AI tools to productize services.
3. Validate demand through small-scale testing.
